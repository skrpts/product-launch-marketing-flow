## Launch Plan Development

This skill assembles the approved positioning and the recommended channel strategy into a single, execution-ready launch marketing plan — the plan the marketing team runs from without further strategic guidance.

### Core Capability

Given the positioning statement and messaging pillars plus the channel strategy recommendations, this skill determines the launch tier, allocates budget and team resources, and lays out the full pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch timeline with owners, milestones, risks, and a RACI matrix.

### Method

1. **Tier and scope:** Determine the launch tier (major / significant / incremental) and set the plan's scope accordingly.
2. **Timeline construction:** Sequence every activity across the pre-launch, launch-week, and post-launch phases against relative milestones (L-6 weeks through L+8 weeks).
3. **Resourcing:** Allocate budget by category and assign responsibility, filling every field with a specific recommendation rather than placeholder text.

### Output Structure

A complete launch plan following the launch-plan-template — overview, phased timeline, budget allocation, risk register, and RACI — ready for stakeholder sign-off. This is the primary deliverable the workflow polishes for release.
