## Press Release Writing

This skill drafts a complete, distribution-ready launch press release that carries the approved positioning into a factual, newsworthy format suitable for journalists and the company newsroom.

### Core Capability

Given the approved positioning statement plus the product and customer details, this skill produces a standard-format release — headline, subheadline, dateline, lead, context, spokesperson quote, product detail, optional customer quote, availability, boilerplate, and media contact — free of marketing jargon and with every claim substantiated or attributed.

### Method

1. **Newsworthiness:** Lead with the genuine news element and answer the five Ws in the opening paragraph.
2. **Substantiation:** Attribute every claim to a quote or a proof point; include at least one supporting data point or market statistic.
3. **Format discipline:** Hold to 400-600 words in third person, factual throughout, and append three accompanying social posts for distribution.

### Output Structure

A complete press release in standard format followed by three suggested social media posts, ready for legal and executive approval ahead of distribution.
